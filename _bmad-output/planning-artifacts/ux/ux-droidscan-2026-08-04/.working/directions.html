droidscan — design directions board

Three complete visual personalities on the same Overview hero. Each differs in density, typography, motion register, and chrome — they are not palette swaps. Below every phone: the glossary bottom sheet (secondary surface) and the Radio permission-denied empty state (failure path).

A · Terminal Purist

btop in your pocket

The phone screen is a terminal emulator — zero chrome, section rules, mono everywhere. This is the strongest anti-*#*#4636#*#* statement: engineers get a tool that already speaks their language, dense enough to be trusted at a glance.

C · Signal Clarity

precision instrument

A precision instrument with calm confidence. The signal story leads as a large readout with its trend line; generous whitespace and a single cyan accent read modern and considered, not hackerly. The least dense, most approachable direction — while never hiding the engineering.

How to read this board — every direction renders the same Overview hero for the same device at the same moment (RSRP −112, IMS not registered, throttling active), so the decision is purely about visual personality. A = densest, most "engineer", lowest polish bar but strongest statement. B = best glanceability and hierarchy, closest to Grafana muscle memory. C = most modern and approachable, biggest delta from the current build, least dense. Pick one direction (or a blend, e.g. "B's cards with A's density") and we promote it — plus the theme from .working/color-themes.html — into DESIGN.md and EXPERIENCE.md.
